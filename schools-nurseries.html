Qualified coaches in your school every week — raising the quality of PE, lifting playground energy and giving teachers their planning time back.

How we support your school

📚

Curriculum PE

National-curriculum-aligned PE lessons planned, delivered and assessed by our coaches, with teacher CPD built in so the impact lasts.

🕐

Lunchtime & After-School Clubs

Football, multi-sport, dodgeball and dance clubs that make active minutes the easiest part of your school day.

🧒

Early Years & Nurseries

Movement, balance and ball-skills sessions designed for ages 2–4 — short, playful and matched to EYFS physical development goals.

🏆

Events & Tournaments

Sports days, inter-school festivals and intra-school competitions organised end-to-end, equipment included.
