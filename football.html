Weekly Football

Term-time sessions where young Royals fall in love with the game — from first touches at four to development squads at fourteen.

Find your level

Sessions for every stage

🐣

Little Kickers (4–6)

Fun-first introductions to the ball: dribbling games, mini matches and lots of high-fives. Parents welcome pitchside.

⚽

Soccer School (6–11)

Weekly technical sessions building passing, control and game understanding, finishing every block with a festival day.

🚀

Development Centres (8–14)

Trial-based squads offering a genuine pathway towards academy football, coached to a structured player-development syllabus.

👧

Girls' Football (5–12)

Dedicated girls-only sessions run by our female coaches — building the next generation of the women's game in Reading.

Our coaching approach

Players first, results second

  • FA-qualified coaches with age-appropriate session plans
  • Maximum touches: small groups, small-sided games
  • Progress tracked and shared with parents each half-term
  • Clear pathway from Little Kickers to Development Centre
  • Respect, effort and enjoyment valued over scorelines
