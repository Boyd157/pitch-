Community & Inclusion

The hoops belong to everyone. Our community programmes use the power of the badge to get people of all ages and abilities moving, connecting and belonging.

Programmes

Something for every Royal

🚶

Walking Football (50+)

The beautiful game at a kinder pace. Friendly weekly sessions for over-50s — all standards, all welcome, tea and chat afterwards guaranteed.

♿

Pan-Disability Football

Inclusive sessions for children and adults with physical, sensory or learning disabilities, adapted by coaches trained in inclusive sport.

💙

Health & Wellbeing

Fitness, weight-management and mental-wellbeing programmes that use football and friendship to make healthy habits stick.

🎉

Matchday & Events

Community takeover days, school holiday festivals and charity tournaments that bring the whole town together around the club.

Our promise

No one watches from the sidelines

  • Free or low-cost entry points for every programme
  • Accessible venues and adapted equipment as standard
  • Coaches trained in inclusive and disability sport
  • Referral routes for GPs, schools and community groups
  • A warm welcome whether you play, volunteer or cheer
