FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What should my child bring to camp?

Trainers, weather-appropriate clothing, a packed lunch, snacks and a filled water bottle. Sun cream and a hat in summer; we'll always email a kit list before camp starts.

What are your staff checks and ratios?

Every coach holds an enhanced DBS check, safeguarding training and paediatric first aid. We staff camps at a maximum of 1 coach to 12 children, lower for our youngest groups.

My child is nervous or hasn't played sport before — will they be okay?

Absolutely. Most of our campers come for fun, not competition. Coaches group children by age and confidence, and our team is great at helping shy arrivals settle in the first hour.

What happens if it rains?

Camps run come rain or shine — every venue has indoor space, and we adapt the day's plan to the weather.

Can I cancel or move a booking?

Bookings can be moved to another date free of charge up to 7 days before camp. Cancellations more than 14 days out receive a full refund; within 14 days we offer credit towards a future booking. (Placeholder policy.)

How do you handle medical needs and allergies?

You'll tell us about medical needs, allergies and emergency contacts at booking. Lead coaches review this before every camp day and medication can be stored and administered per your written instructions.

Still got a question?

Our office team replies within one working day — usually much faster.
