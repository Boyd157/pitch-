A typical camp day

From warm-up to whistle

  • 9:00 — Arrival, registration and free play
  • 9:30 — Warm-up games and team selection
  • 10:00 — Morning activity block (skills & games)
  • 12:00 — Lunch and chill-out time
  • 12:45 — Afternoon tournament or themed challenge
  • 3:15 — Awards, certificates and home time
