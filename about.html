About Us

We believe every child in Reading deserves a brilliant first experience of sport — and that a football club can be the heartbeat of its town far beyond ninety minutes on a Saturday.

Our story

More than a matchday

Reading FC Community exists to put the badge to work for the town: in school halls on a Tuesday morning, on camp pitches in August, and at walking football on a winter evening. Sport changed our coaches' lives — our job is to pass that on.

We started with a single after-school club and a bag of footballs. Today the goal is the same as day one: fun first, every child included, and standards a parent can trust.

What we stand for

Our values

😄

Fun First

Children who enjoy sport stay in sport. Every session is planned around smiles before skills.

🛡️

Safe Always

Safeguarding isn't a policy on a shelf — it's how we recruit, train and run every single session.

🌍

Everyone In

Any age, any ability, any background. If you want to play, we'll find you a place to belong.

📈

Always Improving

Our coaches train as hard as our players — ongoing CPD keeps every session sharp.
