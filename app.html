How it works

Free for every family

The Royals Family Pass is a free app from Reading FC Community Trust, packed with free tickets and family offers across the Thames Valley. No subscriptions, no card details.

Share with another family

Know a family who'd love free tickets and days out? Send them the app link by WhatsApp or text message.

Limited ticket drops

  1. Some offers have a fixed allocation — e.g. the first 100 families for Reading Rockets.
  2. Tap Claim my QR ticket to secure yours. The live counter shows how many are left.
  3. Once the allocation is gone, the offer shows as fully claimed.

At the venue

Open My Tickets and either show the QR code for staff to scan, or switch to Quote code and read out your short ticket code — perfect for venues without a scanner. One redemption per ticket, no printing needed.

For venues & partners

Partners set their own allocation and validity dates. Redemptions are logged so the Trust can report exactly how many families each offer reached.

For the Trust team

Offers, allocations and push alerts are managed in the Trust dashboard. New offers published there appear in this app straight away.
