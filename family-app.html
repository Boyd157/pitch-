New concept · Reading FC Community Trust

The Royals Family Pass app

A free app that puts free tickets and family offers for the whole Thames Valley in every family's pocket — with push alerts the moment a new offer drops, and limited QR tickets you simply show at the venue.

▶ Launch the interactive demo See the launch offers

Try it yourself

A working prototype, not a mock-up

  • 100% free for families — no subscriptions, no card details, just download and go
  • Simple search — type "basketball" or "swim" and the right offer appears instantly
  • Push notifications — families get an alert the moment a new offer becomes available
  • Limited QR drops — e.g. only the first 100 families can download the Reading Rockets ticket; a live counter shows what's left
  • Show at the venue — the QR ticket lives in the app, staff scan it once, no printing

The demo on the right is fully interactive — search the offers, claim a QR ticket, watch a push notification arrive, and try the venue redemption flow. Everything runs in your browser.

The clever bit

How limited QR tickets work

Offer drops

The Trust publishes an offer with a fixed allocation — say, 100 Reading Rockets family tickets. Every family with the app gets a push notification instantly.

Families claim

Tap "Claim my QR ticket". The server allocates a unique, single-use code — one per family — and the live counter ticks down for everyone. When 100 are gone, they're gone.

Show at the venue

On the day, the family opens My Tickets and shows the QR code at the door. Venue staff scan it (or tap "mark as used") — it can only ever be redeemed once.

Trust sees the impact

Every claim and redemption is logged, so the Trust can report exactly how many Thames Valley families each partner offer reached — gold dust for funders and sponsors.

How we'd build it

Simple to launch, ready to grow

  • Phase 1 — launch app: iOS & Android app with offers feed, search, QR wallet and push notifications; lightweight admin dashboard for the Trust to publish offers and set allocations
  • Server-enforced limits: ticket allocations are counted on the server, so "first 100" really means the first 100 — no over-claiming, no duplicate codes
  • Easy for venues: staff scan with any phone camera, or simply sight the valid pass — no hardware to buy
  • Phase 2: per-match ticket selection, waitlists when an offer sells out, family accounts with child ages for age-targeted offers, and partner self-service
