What we do

Four ways to get involved

🏕️

Holiday Camps

Action-packed multi-sport and football camps every school holiday, for ages 4–13. Girls-only weeks, shorter days for our youngest Royals, and HAF-supported places.

Explore camps →
🏫

Schools & Nurseries

Curriculum PE delivery, lunchtime and after-school clubs, and early-years movement sessions led by qualified, DBS-checked coaches.

Partner with us →
⚽

Weekly Football

Term-time soccer schools and player development centres where young Royals build skills, confidence and a love of the game.

Why families choose us

Safe hands, big smiles

  • Every coach is enhanced-DBS checked, first-aid trained and safeguarding qualified
  • Sessions built around fun first — confidence and skills follow
  • Ofsted-registered camps with childcare-voucher and HAF options
  • Small group ratios so every child gets coaching, not crowd control
  • The pride of the hoops: connected to the club your kids already love
